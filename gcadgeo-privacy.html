Privacy Policy — GCAD Geo

Effective date: November 20, 2026 · App: GCAD Geo (vn.aquamep.fieldview, Android) · Developer: Caryna Studio (Individual Developer: Nguyen Trong Gon) · Contact: carynastudio@gmail.com

Terms of Service

1. Summary

  • Your drawings, measurements, notes, photos and GPS/GNSS positions stay on your device. We do not read, upload or sell them.
  • CAD files (DWG, DXF, FVZ) and survey data are processed 100 % on the device and are never uploaded to any server. If a drawing needs more memory than the device has, the app refuses to open it and tells you so; it does not send the file anywhere.
  • Purchases and subscriptions are handled directly by Google Play Billing. We do not operate a licence or payment server for GCAD Geo and we do not receive your purchase token or payment details.
  • GCAD Geo contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, and does not send automatic crash reports.
  • The app makes network requests only for: Google Play, map tiles for the area you are viewing, optional resource packs, and servers you configure yourself (NTRIP/CORS caster, WebDAV storage).
  • You can use the app without an account. Signing in with Google is optional.

5. Security

  • Plan status and credentials are held in Android Keystore-backed storage. Connections to servers use HTTPS.
  • Because CAD files and survey data never leave the device, they cannot be exposed by a server breach of ours.
  • No password is stored for the app itself, because there is no GCAD Geo account.

6. Retention, deletion and your rights

  • On your device: data exists until you delete it (Android Settings → Apps → GCAD Geo → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall).
  • We hold no purchase, payment or account records for GCAD Geo. Purchase records are kept by Google Play under Google's policies.
  • Requesting deletion: if you signed in with Google or sent us a diagnostic file or e-mail, write to carynastudio@gmail.com with the subject "GCAD Geo — data deletion". We delete any data we hold about you within 30 days of receiving the request and confirm by reply. You can also remove the profile stored on the device by signing out or clearing app data.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR/UK GDPR, the CCPA/CPRA or Vietnam's Decree 13/2023/ND-CP) you may have the right to access, correct, delete, port or restrict processing of personal data and to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority. Use the same e-mail address.
  • We do not sell or share personal data for advertising.

7. International transfers

Map tile requests go to the global infrastructure of the providers named above, which may be outside your country. We do not transfer your CAD files or survey data, because they never leave your device.

8. Children

GCAD Geo is a professional tool and is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect their data.
